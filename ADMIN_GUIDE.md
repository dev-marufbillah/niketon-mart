# Admin guide — Niketon Mart

## Sign in and staff

Staff and customers use the common `/my-account/login`; the old `/admin/login` remains a redirect to it. Only a verified active staff password creates an admin session, and ordinary signup never grants admin rights. An authenticated staff member sees an Admin dashboard shortcut on the storefront (on mobile, inside the menu) and can shop using a separate staff-linked customer profile. The protected owner is `marufsalauddin76@gmail.com`; its password is set securely during first bootstrap, never supplied in this guide. Only that owner can manage admin users at `/admin/users`; ordinary admins cannot remove, disable, demote or replace the owner. Direct database access bypasses application protections and must be restricted.

To invite a staff member, configure real Resend email and the correct HTTPS site origin, then use **Admin users → Invite**. Select a non-owner role or permitted custom permissions. The invitation expires after 48 hours and can be used once; the recipient chooses their own password. If delivery fails the account stays inactive and the owner can resend. A provider accepting a message does not prove inbox delivery. Reset links expire after 30 minutes; suspending a user revokes their access. Never ask for or set another person's password.

## Using the admin workspace

Use **Find a section** in the sidebar to find any section allowed by your role; clear the search to see the complete menu. On mobile, open the menu with the top-left button; Escape closes it and returns keyboard focus. Your name/initials open **My account**, while **View shop** opens the storefront in a new tab. Dashboard quick links are limited by your permissions. Dashboard order values include unpaid orders and are not cash collected; chart dates use UTC.

On **Orders**, search and status/unpaid filters work together. In **Inventory → Open products**, the product list is filtered to stock of 10 or fewer, including out-of-stock. Quick stock edits save when the field loses focus; a stale edit is rejected and the latest balance reloaded. In content lists, mobile cards include the same available edit/visibility/reorder actions. In **Site settings**, use **Jump to a section** and then **Save settings**. Permissions and server-side validation still apply to every action. See `ADMIN_DESIGN_UPDATE_BANGLA.md` for the tested scope and remaining limits.

## Homepage and hero slider

The site is a single-store shop: there is no seller/vendor registration or multi-vendor marketplace. Manage the storefront from this one admin. After staff sign-in, open **CMS → Homepage** to reorder or hide homepage sections and edit each section's copy. Open **Hero banner** to control automatic slide rotation and its interval (3–15 seconds). Use **CMS → Hero slides** to add, edit, delete, show/hide and order slides. Each slide supports its own headline, accent, copy, desktop/mobile images, image fit/focus, accessible image description, optional image labels and primary/secondary links. Upload an image or choose one from the media library; save the slide and preview the homepage. The carousel has previous/next and direct slide buttons; it pauses while hovered/focused and respects the shopper's reduced-motion preference. **CMS → Site settings** controls the store identity, site copy, catalogue page descriptions, contact details, announcement, SEO and footer. Products/categories stay connected to the existing Catalogue section.

The storefront navigation order is Home → Shop → Categories → Fresh Market → Grocery → Dates (when the owner features a category) → About Us. Categories follow Shop and About Us remains last; category names and the featured Dates slot use the existing catalogue/admin controls rather than a second menu system. On mobile, the header keeps the brand and search; the bottom bar provides Home, Categories, Bag, Wishlist and Account. Categories opens the existing mobile menu, so the complete catalogue navigation remains available without duplicating actions in the header.

The homepage Fresh Market section shows up to six in-stock products per selected department by default. Change the **Products per department** field in **CMS → Homepage → Fresh market departments** to show fewer; only real catalogue products are displayed.

## Catalogue and fresh market

1. **Categories & brands** (`/admin/catalogue/taxonomy`): add a category or one-level subcategory; edit its name, slug, description, image, visibility, home/navigation flags and sort order. To change a picture, click **Edit**, upload a JPG/PNG/WebP, wait for the upload to finish, then click **Save**. It appears in the desktop and mobile category menus, Categories page and homepage. If no category image is set, a visible product image is used; otherwise a neutral placeholder is shown. Removing an image does not delete the uploaded media file.
   - A top-level **Navigation group** of Fresh Market places that category and its child products on `/fresh-market`. Grocery & Essentials, Household and Bakery place them on `/grocery`. Children follow their current parent. Legacy categories without a recognized group default to Fresh Market; review their groups in admin.
   - A hidden parent hides its children and their products. Turning off “Show in category navigation” hides it and its children from both menus, the primary-category slot and the Categories page, but does not hide products from the shop. Move products/children before deleting a category in use.
   - To show **Dates** in the main navigation, create or edit a top-level category named `Dates`, choose the appropriate group (usually Grocery & Essentials), enable **Show in category navigation** and **Feature in primary navigation**, then save. One category at a time can occupy this slot; first unfeature any current selection. About Us follows this slot on desktop and mobile; Blog stays in the footer.
   - Add each real variety through the same form: enter its name, choose **Dates** as **Parent category**, optionally upload its image, leave **Show in category navigation** enabled and click **Add**. Edit, hide or reorder it in the same panel. Opening Dates displays its visible navigation-enabled children, a truthful zero-product state, and the existing ID-filtered product list. The product count is published, visible products, not a promise of available stock. Other filters are retained when choosing a child. A hidden child and its products disappear; turning off only its navigation checkbox removes the tile but leaves its published products in the shop.
   - In **Products → New product**, select `Dates › [variety]` under **Category / subcategory**. Enter the genuine description, BDT price, stock and image; new products start without a photo, and temporary `blob:`, `file:` and embedded `data:` sources cannot be saved. Save and check both the parent and child storefront views. Image uploads block product submission while in progress. Changing a category name also updates assigned product labels.
   - A fresh local demo has the editable example names Ajwa, Medjool, Mabroom, Safawi and Sukkari under Dates but contains no date inventory. Existing/production databases are never changed or seeded automatically: set up actual categories/products manually after approved deployment, backup and data review.
2. **Products**: create/edit genuine items, selling unit, quantity/stock and applicable variation/preparation groups. For Fresh Fish, enter the verified current market rate as a numeric BDT price for the selected selling unit; if pricing per kg, set both unit fields to `1 kg`. Update the rate manually in Admin—there is no automatic market-price feed. Configure any extra charge there; never add unrelated preparation choices. The server re-prices selections and preserves the chosen options and charge in the order snapshot.
3. **Preparation & cutting**: manage reusable preparation options/presets in the same admin. **Inventory** records staff stock adjustments. Verify physical stock and prices before enabling sales.
4. **Media library**: upload JPG/PNG/WebP (up to 5 MiB in the app; the host may impose a lower request limit). Content is decoded/validated and stored persistently in Blob on Vercel. Do not upload private documents; images are public.
5. **Site settings → Catalogue pages**: staff with `settings.manage` can change each page's heading and introductory/SEO description. The requested English navigation labels and URLs are fixed; product/category content comes from the catalogue, not a separate page database.

## Orders, weighing and payments

**Production checkout remains paused** pending provider integration and real-service staging. Customer self-service cancellation remains unavailable. Staff can review eligible new offline orders that are unpaid, or already have an independently completed and recorded refund; the action requires `orders.manage` and `payments.manage`, plus customer/coupon permissions when relevant. The reviewer must explicitly retain or release coupon usage and either leave customer totals unchanged or enter verified reconciled totals. Order, inventory, coupon/customer changes and the private review record commit in one MongoDB transaction. Paid/unrefunded, gateway-unpaid, historical, or otherwise unreconciled orders remain held. Local test orders are not real orders.

For a variable-weight order, enter the actual grams on its admin order page. A lower weight may reduce a reconcilable unpaid total. A higher amount is a proposal: the customer reviews and approves the exact new total on their private order page. Staff may waive the increase but cannot approve it on the customer's behalf. Paid, transfer-awaiting-verification, historically incomplete or unreconciled orders are held for manual review. Tax, delivery and the original checkout discount are snapshotted; do not guess missing historical amounts.

The payment screen records staff decisions; it does **not** transfer money or process a gateway transaction. Independently check the amount and payment reference. Record a refund only after an actual refund, for a paid order, with a separate refund reference and verification note. Preserve evidence outside the application as required by your accounting process. Gateway methods/callbacks are not implemented, regardless of credentials.

## Content and customers

Use Homepage, Top Picks, pages, posts, banners, FAQ and Settings in the same admin to maintain real content. In **Homepage → Our Top Picks**, choose and order products, edit the copy and save; removing the optional button label/link removes that button. Changes appear on a subsequent storefront request. Delivery areas, charges, partners and payment methods are under Operations. Contact messages and newsletter subscriptions are saved in admin; their existence does not mean an email/SMS was sent. Reviews need moderation and only genuine approved reviews appear publicly. Customer access is scoped to their own authenticated order or a private guest key; an order number plus phone/email is not sufficient.

Review the Activity screen for changes. The current logging is best-effort, so it is not a guaranteed or tamper-proof audit record. See `REMAINING_TASKS.md` before launch.
